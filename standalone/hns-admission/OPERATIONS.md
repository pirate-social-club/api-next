# Runtime configuration and qualification

Alchemy owns this standalone stack, its Worker, Domain Durable Objects and deployment state. The service has no EVM private key. Do not upgrade the Wizard or route this package through its deployment. The exact installed Alchemy version is2.0.0-beta.87 and Effect is4.1.4. Portability beyond Cloudflare is untested.

The required environment is HNS_ADMISSION_MANIFEST plus the two redacted secrets HNS_OBSERVER_A_AUTHORIZATION and HNS_OBSERVER_B_AUTHORIZATION. src/config.ts is the closed configuration schema. The manifest supplies HNS network/genesis/tree interval, two distinct observer URLs, up to 50 exact access profiles and publicationEnabled. Each profile fixes network, TLD, chain, registry, wrapper, controller, community and source digest. It does not silently approve a deployment for another controller or target. Mainnet observers require HTTPS. Keep publicationEnabled false until the live registry/community/controller and deployment/encoding review is complete.

Use managed credentials at deployment time, including the Infisical boundary. Cloudflare membership, deployment credentials and Infisical access can expose or replace Worker credentials; give each the smallest required scope. This slice adds no credential, account or Infisical integration. An Alchemy plan/deployment is a separate reviewed operation. The project-local CLI is npm exec -- alchemy; consult its help rather than guessing flags. No cf or Wrangler path is used for this package.

Each observer must have transaction indexing and sufficient retained covenant history. The reader walks at most 64 ownership covenants by default and fails unavailable if history is absent or too deep. Two URLs are checked for equality, but deployment review must establish that they are genuinely different nodes. Never give the Worker an unfiltered all-method HSD credential. Put a read-only allowlisting proxy in front of each node for getblockchaininfo, getblockhash, getblockheader, getnameinfo and getrawtransaction. The client also restricts those methods. Installing or changing those proxies is outside this slice.

The reader permits five seconds per RPC and thirty seconds per node snapshot, rejects redirects and bounds each JSON response at 1 MiB. A snapshot is bracketed by an unchanged canonical tip; current and safe name views are checked independently. Different tips, roots, reported tip/inclusion timestamps, ownership epochs or resource data mean unavailable, including when one view is merely catching up. Current/safe ownership disagreement remains unavailable until stable, including after provisional FINALIZE or temporary absence. Freshness is checked before an absent-name decision. Never pick one observer. Agreement older than six hours is chain_freshness_unconfirmed. A tip more than 120 seconds ahead or an observation fetched more than 60 seconds ago is unavailable. These are conservative decision gates, not negative proofs.

No minimum remaining renewal interval is required. A currently controlled, unexpired name can verify immediately before its renewal boundary. The service does not schedule renewals, impose a renewal-date cutoff or use a historical renewal timestamp as authority. Already expired or transferred names cannot establish current control. This is distinct from the later contract attestation freshness lease.

A challenge has 48 hours for publication. Once timely canonical inclusion is established, a separate 24-hour maturity window applies; publication near hour45 retains that window. Service consent lasts at most 72 hours and may be renewed without republishing. Mainnet uses the pinned HSD safe-root entry rule with interval 36 and its 12-block branch, together with matching safe/current resource and ownership epochs. Regtest uses its actual interval 5; mainnet boundary vectors are tested separately. A delayed wake compares stored timestamps. Missing TXT remains publication_pending, not rejected. Cancel is terminal. A finalized ownership change suspends the operation. Pending transfer prevents readiness. A reorg or missing record removes current readiness until re-observation.

Known outages self-schedule after 60, 120, 240 and then 300 seconds until the applicable deadline. A durable 45-second reservation coalesces concurrent probes. Before external I/O, the observation policy also arms a five-minute continuation. ReadyToSubmit stops polling but retains a consent-deadline wake; a later executor must independently re-observe. Reads advance deadlines but never sign or broadcast. Unexpected crashes still require deployed recovery qualification; local in-memory success is not that qualification.

The enabled HTTP capabilities are ceremony creation, consent, authorized status, observation and cancellation. GET /v1/capabilities lists disabled admission, refresh, installation-proof, submission, reconciliation and sender-recovery effects. Mainnet consent is refused while publicationEnabled is false. Initial creation and later operation reads are signed without a separate Pirate account. Status exposes only the bound operation after authorization. Responses include absolute deadlines, inclusion/maturity, retryAt, typed conditions and public binding/observation content. There are no receipts or authority grants in this slice.

One canonical pd1 per chain may coexist with records for other chains when capacity permits. _evm still chooses exactly one active chain/registry. Conflicts return delegation_conflict with duplicate_chain, malformed_record, ambiguous_routing or active_registry_mismatch. A valid inactive chain returns delegation_target_inactive. A retained pc1 returns delegation_conflict/challenge_present: cancel or reconcile its previous ceremony before manually removing/replacing that freshness record and starting another. No record is silently deleted. After future successful admission, pc1 may be removed; persistent pd1 and routing remain. That maintenance path and refresh executor are deferred.

One admitted installation per namespace is the pilot limit. Admission, issuer rotation, rebind, submitter switching and governance recovery remain wrapper/consumer work. The per-community installation key will sign member grants; this service does not decide community membership or sign member grants. Sponsorship budgets and gas payment are not part of these read-only HTTP operations. Alerts to workspace_owner, including observer failures and low sponsor funds, belong to the later operational/sponsorship slice.

Before deployed use, independently review source, run the parent repository gates, discover actual DO exports in an Alchemy staging plan, and test a deployed staging Worker across prolonged observer failure, eviction and crash boundaries. The later wrapper/sponsorship review must precede any real signing or submission. Freeze the real publication target before the authorized manual operator-wallet ceremony; preserve its existing NS/DS resource. Existing ownership or historical receipts must never be erased by later freshness/revocation decisions. Linux failed-install reconciliation remains a separate integration gap.
